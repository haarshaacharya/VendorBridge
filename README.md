# VendorBridge – Procurement & Vendor Management ERP

VendorBridge is a scalable, role-based Procurement & Vendor Management ERP designed to digitize and automate end-to-end procurement workflows including vendor onboarding, RFQ management, quotation handling, approval systems, purchase order generation, and invoicing.

The platform focuses on improving transparency, reducing manual procurement overhead, and enabling structured enterprise-grade workflows.

---

## 🚀 Product Vision

To build a centralized procurement system that enables organizations to manage vendors, procurement requests, approvals, and financial documents in a seamless and traceable workflow.

VendorBridge aims to replace fragmented manual procurement processes with a unified digital ERP system.

---

## 🧩 Core Features

### 🔐 Authentication & Access Control
- Secure login / signup system
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Session management
- Password recovery flow (optional)

---

### 📊 Dashboard
- Real-time procurement overview
- Pending approvals
- Active RFQs
- Recent purchase orders & invoices
- Quick action shortcuts
- Basic analytics cards

---

### 🏢 Vendor Management
- Vendor onboarding and profile management
- GST, contact, and category tracking
- Vendor status monitoring (Active / Inactive / Blacklisted)
- Search and filtering system

---

### 📩 RFQ (Request for Quotation)
- Create and manage RFQs
- Add product/service specifications
- Quantity and deadline configuration

- Attachment support

---

### 💬 Quotation Management
- Vendor quotation submission portal
- Price, delivery timeline, and remarks
- Editable quotations before submission
- Centralized quotation storage

---

### ⚖️ Quotation Comparison Engine
- Side-by-side comparison view
- Price-based ranking
- Delivery timeline comparison
- Vendor rating indicators (optional)
- Smart sorting & filtering

---

### ✅ Approval Workflow System
- Multi-stage approval flow
- Approve / reject with comments
- Approval history tracking
- Status lifecycle management

---

### 🧾 Purchase Order & Invoice Module
- Auto-generated Purchase Order (PO)
- Conversion from approved quotations
- Tax and total calculation engine
- Invoice generation system
- Download as PDF
- Print invoice support
- Email invoice to vendors/clients

---

### 📡 Activity Logs & Notifications
- Real-time procurement updates
- Audit logs for all actions
- RFQ / PO / Invoice tracking
- System notifications

---

### 📈 Reports & Analytics
- Procurement spending analysis
- Vendor performance insights
- Monthly / yearly trends
- Exportable reports (PDF/CSV)

## 👥 User Roles & Permissions

### 🛠 Admin
- User management
- Vendor management
- System-wide analytics access

### 🧑‍💼 Procurement Officer
- Create RFQs
- Manage quotations
- Generate purchase orders & invoices

### 🧑‍⚖️ Manager / Approver
- Approve / reject procurement requests
- Monitor workflow states
- Add approval comments

### 🏭 Vendor
- Receive RFQs
- Submit quotations
- Track RFQ / PO status
